# 📢 Share Repository with Your Team (Read-Only Access)

## 🌐 Make Your Repository Public (Recommended for Viewing/Cloning)

### Step 1: Make Repository Public

1. Go to your repository: **https://github.com/ShashiSwarnkar/MSAI_631**
2. Click **Settings** (top right)
3. Scroll down to **Danger Zone** (at the bottom)
4. Click **Change visibility**
5. Select **Make public**
6. Confirm by typing the repository name

✅ **Done!** Now anyone can view and clone your repository.

## 🔍 What Your Team Can Do (Without Being Collaborators)

### ✅ They CAN:
- **View all files** on GitHub (in their browser)
- **Clone the repository** to their computer
- **Download as ZIP** (no Git needed)
- **Fork the repository** (create their own copy)
- **Create Issues** (report bugs/suggestions)
- **Read all documentation**
- **Run the code locally** after cloning

### ❌ They CANNOT:
- **Push changes** directly to your repository
- **Create branches** on your repository
- **Merge Pull Requests**
- **Modify repository settings**

## 🎯 How Team Members Can Contribute (Without Collaborator Access)

### Method 1: Fork & Pull Request (Recommended)

1. **Fork** your repository (creates their own copy)
2. **Clone** their fork to their computer
3. **Make changes** in their fork
4. **Create a Pull Request** to your repository
5. **You review and merge** their changes

This is the standard open-source workflow!

### Method 2: Download & Share Changes

1. **Download** the repository as ZIP
2. **Make changes** locally
3. **Share** their modified files with you
4. **You manually merge** their changes
